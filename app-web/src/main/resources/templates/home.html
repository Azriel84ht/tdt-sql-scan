Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Data Flow Visualizer?

It is a software tool that analyzes Teradata BTEQ script files and automatically generates a visual and interactive graph. This graph helps you understand the data flow and execution logic of your ETL processes in a much more intuitive way than reading the code.

Do I need to install anything on my servers?

No. Our application works completely independently. You just need to upload your script files (.bteq) through our web interface. We do not require access to your databases or your systems.

Is it safe to upload our scripts?

Yes. Security is our top priority. Scripts are processed in memory on our secure servers and are not stored permanently unless you decide so. We use end-to-end encryption to protect your data.
